Excluded segments

Stretches of the recording to leave out of the analysis, for example where the image moved. The movie is not cut: its frames, the Suite2p traces, the spikes and your cleaning stay as they are, and nothing before the analysis is redone. The analysis leaves the frames out: no spike whose window (from before to after the spike) overlaps them is used, no chance copy is drawn from them, and the image shifts inside them do not count for the border the test leaves out. Hypotheses that were run before have to be run again (they say so below). Times are seconds of the recording shown in the traces of this tab.

Counts are not compared as they are: a class with more cells has more chances for a significant cell. Each class shows the fraction of its tested cells that are significant, with a 95% interval, and a test against the false-positive rate alone ({{ "%g"|format(class_stats.alpha) }} of cells expected). The two classes are compared by Fisher's exact test (it allows for different group sizes) and by a permutation test that keeps the group sizes and shuffles the class only among cells with a similar number of spikes (cells with more spikes are more likely to be significant, and the classes may differ in that). Uncertain and unclassified cells are shown and not compared.

Cells with significant clusters

Loading...

Raw traces: choose a cell in the dropdown (only cells with a significant cluster are listed); a full-width plot per significant cluster of that cell shows the mean intensity of the cluster's pixels in every frame of the recording (red = depolarizing cluster, blue = hyperpolarizing; solid = all result type, dashed = anatomy; depolarization is up) with the spikes of the cell as dashed white vertical lines. The plots of a cell share the time: zoom or pan the time in one and the others follow, and each height fits the part of the trace that is shown (zoom the height by hand and it stays until the time changes). Cards: one card per cell with the mean astro field of view (the cell's ROI in yellow, its clusters outlined, a ring on each), the clusters' mean traces and the cell's own spike-triggered neuron dF/F (yellow, right axis), and under it, for each cluster, individual spikes: the mean of the cluster's pixels around every spike of the cell (thin) with their mean (bold), next to the same for the pixels right around the cluster (as many as the cluster has), on the same scale.

Zoom: the mouse wheel zooms around the pointer; over the left axis it zooms the height only, over the time axis the time only (Shift: time only, Alt: height only anywhere in the plot); Shift + drag draws a box and zooms to it, as wide and as tall as you draw it (a thin box zooms one axis); drag pans; double click goes back to the whole plot. The field of view zooms with its proportions kept. Click the cell number on a card to open the cell.
