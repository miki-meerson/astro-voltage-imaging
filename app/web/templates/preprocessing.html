ROI types

Open this tab to load the ROIs.

Only the accepted Suite2p ROIs (iscell = 1) are drawn. A ROI's type is an extra label saved by its original Suite2p index: iscell.npy, stat.npy and the cell numbers of the spikes and analyses are not changed. Suite2p has no soma / dendrite classifier, so the automatic rule uses the shape of each ROI (elongation and solidity, measured on a cleaned copy of it). It is untested on your data: ROIs it cannot tell apart are uncertain, not forced into a class. Click a ROI to set its type yourself. Everything is saved in one file; running the automatic classification again replaces all of it, your own labels too.
